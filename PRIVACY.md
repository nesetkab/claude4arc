# Privacy policy

claude4arc is a browser extension, a native messaging host, and a command-line
tool that run on your own computer. This policy covers all three.

## What claude4arc does with data

- The extension connects only to the claude4arc native host on the same
  computer, through the browser's native messaging API. It opens no network
  connections of its own.
- It reads and controls browser tabs only when a program on your computer asks
  it to, through the native host. That program is usually Claude Code, running
  on your behalf.
- Page content, screenshots, and task records stay on your computer, in
  `~/.arc-bridge`. Screenshots older than one day are deleted automatically.

## What claude4arc does not do

- It collects no analytics or telemetry.
- It sends no data to the author or to any third party.
- It does not sell or share data.

What Claude Code does with page content that it reads is covered by the terms
and privacy policy of the Claude product that you use.
